# Project invitations

## Why

Good work starts with the right people. Let project owners invite teammates directly into their workspace, with a clear role and a welcoming first step.

## What changes

- Invite teammates by email, with **editor** or **viewer** access.
- Show pending invitations in the project’s people settings.
- Let owners revoke invitations before they are accepted.
- Expire unused invitations after seven days.

## Capabilities

| Capability | Change |
| --- | --- |
| Project membership | Add invitations and acceptance |
| Access control | Respect the role selected by the owner |

## Impact

This change touches project settings, the invitation service, and the welcome flow. Existing members keep their current access.

Read the [design](design.md) for the implementation approach, or explore the [requirements](specs/projects/membership/spec.md).
